HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY

Document Title: Employee Human Resources Policy
Company: CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Document Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 07 August 2026

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a technology company focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, software engineering, data analytics, and cloud-based technology solutions.

The company is committed to creating an inclusive, professional, innovative, and employee-friendly workplace. This HR Policy establishes the general rules, procedures, benefits, responsibilities, and expectations applicable to employees of CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.


2. PURPOSE OF THE HR POLICY

The purpose of this policy is to provide employees with clear information regarding:

- Working hours and attendance
- Leave and holidays
- Remote and hybrid work
- Employee conduct
- Compensation and payroll
- Performance evaluation
- Promotions
- Training and development
- Workplace communication
- Information security
- Employee benefits
- Grievance procedures
- Resignation and termination
- Workplace health and safety

This policy is intended to provide general guidance. Specific employment terms may also be governed by an employee's appointment letter, employment agreement, or applicable company policies.


3. EMPLOYEE WORKING HOURS

The standard working schedule at CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is Monday to Friday.

Standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees are expected to complete eight working hours per day, excluding the standard lunch break.

The standard lunch break is from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.

Employees are expected to report to work on time and remain available during their scheduled working hours.

Employees who expect to arrive late must inform their reporting manager as early as possible.


4. ATTENDANCE POLICY

Employees must maintain regular and punctual attendance.

Attendance may be recorded through the company's attendance management system, employee portal, biometric system, or another system designated by the HR Department.

Employees must accurately record their working hours.

Repeated unauthorized absence, late attendance, or early departure may result in corrective action.

If an employee is unable to attend work due to an emergency or unexpected situation, the employee should notify the reporting manager and HR Department as soon as reasonably possible.


5. REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. may allow eligible employees to work remotely or under a hybrid work arrangement depending on their role and business requirements.

Remote work approval must be obtained from the employee's reporting manager.

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain the same level of productivity, communication, professionalism, and availability expected from office-based employees.

Employees must ensure that company data is accessed only through approved devices, networks, and security mechanisms.

Remote employees must attend scheduled meetings and remain reachable during their designated working hours.


6. LEAVE POLICY

Employees are eligible for different categories of leave based on their employment type and applicable company rules.

The general leave categories are:

- Casual Leave
- Sick Leave
- Earned Leave
- Public Holidays
- Maternity Leave
- Paternity Leave
- Emergency Leave

Leave requests should normally be submitted through the company's leave management system.

Employees should obtain approval from their reporting manager before taking planned leave.

Emergency or medical leave may be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible when advance approval is not practical.


7. CASUAL LEAVE

Casual Leave may be used for personal requirements, short-term personal matters, or unexpected situations.

Employees are generally eligible for 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year.

Casual Leave should normally be requested at least one working day in advance.

Unused Casual Leave may not automatically carry forward to the following year unless specifically permitted by company policy.


8. SICK LEAVE

Sick Leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or a medical condition.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when taking Sick Leave.

For extended periods of absence, the HR Department may request appropriate medical documentation.

Employees should not work while seriously ill if doing so could negatively affect their health or workplace safety.


9. EARNED LEAVE

Employees may receive 18 days of Earned Leave per calendar year, subject to eligibility and applicable company rules.

Earned Leave should generally be planned in advance and approved by the reporting manager.

Employees are encouraged to plan longer periods of leave sufficiently in advance to minimize disruption to business operations.

The carry-forward or encashment of unused Earned Leave will be governed by the company's applicable leave rules.


10. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. observes public and national holidays according to the company's annual holiday calendar.

The HR Department publishes the official holiday calendar at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees working on designated holidays may be eligible for compensatory leave or other applicable benefits according to company policy.


11. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

Eligible employees may receive maternity and paternity leave in accordance with applicable laws and company policy.

Employees requesting parental leave should contact the HR Department as early as possible.

HR will provide information regarding eligibility, required documentation, leave duration, and the applicable process.


12. COMPENSATION AND SALARY

Employee compensation is determined based on role, skills, experience, responsibilities, market conditions, and organizational requirements.

Salary is normally processed monthly.

Employees are responsible for providing accurate bank and personal information required for payroll processing.

Any salary-related concerns should be reported to the HR Department or Payroll Team.


13. PAYROLL DATE

Monthly salaries are normally credited on or before the last working day of each month.

Payroll processing may be affected by public holidays, banking holidays, or unforeseen technical issues.

Employees should report salary discrepancies to HR within a reasonable period after receiving their salary.


14. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. conducts periodic performance evaluations to assess employee contribution, professional development, and achievement of assigned objectives.

Performance evaluations may consider:

- Quality of work
- Productivity
- Technical skills
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Problem-solving
- Initiative
- Professional conduct
- Achievement of assigned goals
- Contribution to organizational objectives

Employees may receive formal performance reviews annually or at other intervals determined by the company.


15. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN

Employees whose performance does not meet established expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

A PIP may include:

- Specific performance concerns
- Expected improvements
- Measurable objectives
- Support or training provided by the company
- Review periods
- Follow-up evaluations

The duration and conditions of a PIP may vary depending on the employee's role and circumstances.


16. PROMOTION POLICY

Promotions are based on factors such as:

- Performance
- Skills and competencies
- Experience
- Leadership ability
- Role requirements
- Business needs
- Consistent achievement of objectives

Promotion decisions are made by management in consultation with the relevant department and HR team.

Employees are not automatically entitled to promotion based solely on length of service.


17. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. encourages continuous learning and professional development.

Employees may receive access to:

- Technical training
- Soft-skills training
- Leadership development
- Industry certifications
- Internal workshops
- Knowledge-sharing sessions
- Mentorship programs

Employees are encouraged to discuss career development goals with their managers and HR.


18. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully at all times.

Employees must:

- Treat colleagues with respect
- Maintain professional communication
- Follow company policies
- Protect company property
- Maintain confidentiality
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Follow applicable laws and regulations
- Maintain workplace professionalism

Unprofessional, abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or fraudulent behavior may result in disciplinary action.


19. ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and inappropriate behavior.

Harassment may include unwanted verbal, physical, visual, or digital behavior that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

Employees who experience or witness workplace harassment are encouraged to report the matter to HR or through the designated grievance mechanism.

Complaints will be handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.


20. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. provides equal employment opportunities and does not support unlawful discrimination in recruitment, employment, compensation, training, promotion, or other employment-related decisions.

Employment decisions are based on qualifications, skills, performance, role requirements, and legitimate business considerations.


21. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with their responsibilities to CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Potential conflicts of interest should be disclosed to the reporting manager or HR Department.

Examples may include:

- Financial interests that affect business decisions
- Working for a direct competitor without approval
- Using company resources for personal commercial activities
- Accepting inappropriate benefits from vendors
- Participating in decisions involving close personal relationships


22. CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees may have access to confidential company information during their employment.

Confidential information may include:

- Source code
- Software architecture
- Customer information
- Business strategies
- Financial information
- Employee information
- Product roadmaps
- Research and development information
- Security credentials
- Internal documentation

Employees must not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals or external organizations.

Confidentiality obligations may continue after an employee leaves the company where legally applicable.


23. INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees must follow the company's information security requirements.

Employees must:

- Use strong passwords
- Protect authentication credentials
- Avoid sharing passwords
- Lock devices when unattended
- Use approved software
- Report suspected security incidents
- Avoid unauthorized access to systems
- Follow company data protection procedures

Employees must immediately report lost devices, suspected account compromise, phishing attempts, or unauthorized access to the IT or Security Team.


24. COMPANY DEVICES AND ASSETS

Company-owned devices and assets must be used responsibly.

Company assets may include:

- Laptops
- Mobile devices
- Software licenses
- Access cards
- Cloud accounts
- Development environments
- Other technology equipment

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of assigned company property.

Company assets must be returned when employment ends or when requested by the company.


25. ACCEPTABLE USE OF TECHNOLOGY

Company technology resources should primarily be used for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must not use company systems for:

- Illegal activities
- Unauthorized access
- Malware distribution
- Harassment
- Unauthorized data extraction
- Circumventing security controls
- Activities that could damage company systems or reputation

Limited personal use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work responsibilities or violate company policies.


26. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND GENERATIVE AI USAGE

Employees may use approved Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI tools for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must not submit confidential, proprietary, personal, customer, or sensitive company information to external AI systems unless the tool has been explicitly approved by the company.

AI-generated content must be reviewed by the employee before being used for business purposes.

Employees remain responsible for the accuracy, security, legality, and appropriateness of AI-assisted work.

AI tools must not be used to make unauthorized decisions involving employees, customers, or other individuals.


27. SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY

Employees may use social media in their personal capacity.

Employees must not represent personal opinions as official company statements unless authorized to do so.

Employees must not disclose confidential company information through social media.

Employees should maintain professional conduct when publicly identifying themselves as employees of CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.


28. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Employees may be reimbursed for legitimate business expenses incurred while performing authorized company activities.

Examples may include:

- Business travel
- Client meetings
- Approved training
- Business-related transportation
- Approved accommodation
- Other authorized business expenses

Employees must submit valid receipts and supporting documentation according to the company's expense reimbursement process.


29. BUSINESS TRAVEL

Business travel must normally be approved by the appropriate manager before booking.

Employees are expected to select reasonable and cost-effective travel options.

Travel expenses must comply with the company's approved reimbursement limits.

Employees should submit travel expense claims within the period specified by the Finance Department.


30. EMPLOYEE GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Employees who have concerns regarding workplace issues may raise a grievance with their reporting manager or HR Department.

A grievance may relate to:

- Workplace conduct
- Compensation
- Work environment
- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Managerial concerns
- Policy violations
- Other employment-related matters

Employees should provide relevant information and supporting evidence when submitting a grievance.

HR will review the matter and determine appropriate next steps.


31. WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY

Employees may report suspected fraud, corruption, serious misconduct, financial irregularities, security violations, or other serious policy breaches through the designated reporting channel.

Reports may be submitted to HR, senior management, or another authorized reporting mechanism.

Retaliation against an employee who raises a genuine concern in good faith is not permitted.


32. WORKPLACE HEALTH AND SAFETY

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is committed to maintaining a safe and healthy workplace.

Employees must follow applicable workplace safety procedures.

Employees should immediately report workplace hazards, accidents, injuries, or unsafe conditions to their manager or HR Department.

Emergency procedures and evacuation instructions must be followed during emergencies.


33. PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally through email, messaging platforms, meetings, documentation, and other communication channels.

Employees should avoid offensive, discriminatory, threatening, or inappropriate language.

Important business decisions should be documented through appropriate company communication channels when required.


34. EMPLOYEE PRIVACY

The company may collect and process employee information for legitimate employment, administrative, payroll, legal, security, and operational purposes.

Employees are expected to provide accurate information required for employment administration.

Access to employee information should be limited to authorized personnel based on legitimate business requirements.


35. RESIGNATION

Employees who wish to resign must submit a formal resignation according to the terms of their employment agreement.

The applicable notice period will be specified in the employee's appointment letter or employment contract.

During the notice period, employees are expected to complete assigned responsibilities and support an orderly transition.


36. NOTICE PERIOD

The standard notice period for full-time employees is 30 days unless a different period is specified in the employee's employment agreement.

Employees requesting early release must obtain approval from the appropriate management authority and HR Department.

The company may approve or reject early release requests based on business requirements and applicable employment terms.


37. EXIT PROCESS

Employees leaving CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. must complete the applicable exit formalities.

Exit activities may include:

- Knowledge transfer
- Return of company assets
- Removal of system access
- Completion of pending documentation
- Exit interview
- Final payroll processing
- Clearance from relevant departments

The HR Department will provide employees with information regarding the required exit process.


38. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

Employment may be terminated according to the employee's employment agreement and applicable law.

Reasons for termination may include:

- Serious misconduct
- Policy violations
- Persistent performance issues
- Unauthorized absence
- Fraud
- Breach of confidentiality
- Security violations
- Other legitimate employment-related reasons

The applicable process will depend on the circumstances and relevant employment terms.


39. FINAL SETTLEMENT

After completion of the exit process, the company will process the employee's final settlement according to applicable company procedures and legal requirements.

Final settlement may include eligible salary, approved reimbursements, leave-related payments where applicable, and other amounts due under the employment agreement.


40. POLICY VIOLATIONS

Violation of company policies may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, actions may include:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Performance improvement measures
- Suspension where applicable
- Termination of employment
- Legal action where appropriate

The company will consider the circumstances of each case when determining appropriate action.


41. POLICY REVIEW

The HR Department will periodically review this policy to ensure that it remains relevant and aligned with organizational requirements and applicable laws.

CognivexaAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to modify, update, or replace this policy when necessary.

Employees will be informed of significant policy changes through appropriate internal communication channels.
